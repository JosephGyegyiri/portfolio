About Joseph

Engineering, data, and geospatial analysis for complex water systems.

I am an Assistant Engineer at Hazen and Sawyer specializing in hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, engineering design, and data-driven analysis for large-scale water resources projects.

My work focuses on integrating surface and groundwater systems to support flood-risk assessment, infrastructure resilience, and watershed-scale decision-making. I am particularly interested in applying data science and geospatial analytics to improve engineering workflows and model performance.
