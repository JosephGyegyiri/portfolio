Featured research

IanBD disaster-damage dataset

Co-developed a DOI-registered, openly accessible dataset on NSF-supported DesignSafe for building-damage assessment after Hurricane Ian.

What the dataset contains

  • 101 paired pre- and post-disaster image tiles.
  • Building-level annotations across four standardized damage classes.
  • Geospatially aligned imagery and machine-learning-ready masks.
  • Quality-reviewed data for damage-assessment research.

Use of the dataset

The dataset can be used for model testing, post-disaster analysis, and infrastructure-resilience research. It has also been cited in peer-reviewed research on disaster-imagery damage labels.
